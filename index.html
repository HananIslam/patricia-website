My coaching method

How we get you ready for a difficult conversation

  1. Learn it

    We start with a proven conversation model, so you have a clear structure to follow instead of guessing what to say.

    1

  2. Adapt it

    We shape the model around your real situation: the person, the history between you and the outcome you want.

    2

  3. Practice it

    We rehearse it together, out loud, until the words feel like your own and you're comfortable saying them.

    3

  4. Use it

    You walk into the real conversation prepared, with a plan you've already practised.

What happens on the free intro call?

It's a relaxed 30-minute conversation about where you are and where you want to go. No pressure and no obligation.

Is it normal to find the first year this hard?

Very. Between 60 and 73 percent of new managers struggle in their first year or two (Forbes, 2023 and J-Train, 2025). It usually means you weren't trained, not that you aren't suited to leading.

Who is the coaching for?

New managers in their first year or two, in any industry. People skills are universal.

How long does the coaching take?

The New Leader's Toolkit is 12 one-on-one sessions over 6 months, and you set the pace.

How much does it cost?

We talk through pricing on your intro call, once I understand what you need. Payment plans are available.

Do we meet online or in person?

Sessions run online by video call, so you can join from anywhere in Canada.

Is the self-assessment really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. Your personalised results arrive by email, with a short video from me.
